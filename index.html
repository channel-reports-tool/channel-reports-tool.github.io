Youtube Analytics

A private tool by Tahgi Turner

This is the home page for "Youtube Analytics", a small tool Tahgi Turner uses to read the statistics of his own YouTube channel, @TahgiTurner.

It connects to Google with read-only access and pulls the channel's own reports: views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, subscriber changes and estimated revenue. He uses those reports to plan and improve his videos. It can also upload his finished videos to his own channel as private drafts.

The tool is not a public product. It has one user, the channel's owner, and it cannot change anything on the channel.
