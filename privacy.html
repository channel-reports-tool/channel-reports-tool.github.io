Privacy Policy

App: Youtube Analytics · Operated by Tahgi Turner · Effective November 27, 2026

"Youtube Analytics" is a private tool that Tahgi Turner built to read the statistics of his own YouTube channel, @TahgiTurner. It is not offered to the public and has one user: the channel's owner.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's consent, the app uses Google's YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API with read-only access to:

  • the channel's own video list and public video details (titles, lengths, publish dates);
  • the channel's own analytics reports (views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, subscriber changes);
  • the channel's own estimated revenue and ad performance reports.

With the consent of the channel owner, the app can also upload a finished video file to the channel as a private draft, with its title and description. It uploads as private only; making a video public stays with the channel owner in YouTube Studio. The app cannot delete videos, post or reply to comments, or change any channel or account setting. It does not read private messages, email, contacts or files.

Whose data this is

All of it belongs to the channel owner. YouTube's reports are aggregated, so the app does not receive the names, accounts or contact details of individual viewers, and it does not collect any information from people who visit this page or watch the channel.

How the data is used

Only to plan, review and improve the channel's own videos: seeing which videos held viewers, where they stopped watching, and what each video earned.

Where the data is kept and who sees it

Reports are saved on the channel owner's own computer. To help analyze them, the owner uses AI assistant software (Claude, made by Anthropic), which processes the reports on his behalf. The data is not sold, not shared with advertisers or data brokers, and not used for any purpose other than the one described above.

Keeping and deleting data

Saved reports stay on the owner's computer until he deletes them. The app's access can be removed at any time from the Google Account permissions page, myaccount.google.com/permissions; after that the app can no longer read anything.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Changes

If the app starts doing anything different with this data, this page will be updated first, with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on the About tab of the @TahgiTurner YouTube channel.
